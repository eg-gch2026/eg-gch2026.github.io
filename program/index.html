Social Event: Gaudí in Context – Eixample & Modernisme

Passeig de Gràcia Monday, November 2nd • 18:30 – 20:15

A guided walking tour through the heart of the Eixample, the 19th-century city extension designed by Ildefons Cerdà, following the rise of Catalan Modernisme and the work of Antoni Gaudí, Josep Puig i Cadafalch and Lluís Domènech i Montaner. The tour includes exterior visits to La Pedrera, Casa Batlló and Casa Amatller, among other points of interest.

The visit is organised in 5 predefined groups, each with a specialised English-speaking guide and a GCH contact person. Attendees from the same institution have been placed in the same group. Group assignments will be shared with registered attendees before the conference. We strongly request that you stay in the group you are assigned to.

Important information

  • Meeting time: 18:30. Please arrive at your assigned meeting point on time.
  • Tour: starts at 18:35 and ends at 20:15.
  • Language: English.
  • Audio equipment: personal audio guide receivers will be provided at the beginning of the tour. Please return them to your guide at the end.
